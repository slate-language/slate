# 2026-09-24 — `sh.sysl.gc` 0.2.7: the sweep is one walk of the block, in address order

**gc 0.2.7 frees the dead, relinks the survivors and rebuilds the free lists in ONE pass over the
block, where 0.2.6 took two.** The sweep used to follow the object list — allocation order, which once
storage is being reused is scattered across the block, so every step was a dependent load from
wherever the next object happened to be — and then `rebuild_free` walked the block from its base to
the bump pointer anyway, to merge neighbouring free blocks. That second walk already reads every
header, so the sweep is now that walk: a dead object is finalized (only where its kind has a
`finalize`) and joins the current free run; a live one has its mark cleared and is relinked onto the
object list in the order met; each run is closed into its bin as before. Nothing in slate changed but
the pin. **`csv` −8.7%, `reals` −5.3%, `alloc` −5.1%, `calls` −4.9%, geometric mean −1.26%.**

## Why the finalizer call was not the lever

The item that asked for this (`2026-09-24-object-layout.md` § what is left) named *the sweep with its
finalizer* as the cost after the lookups. Measured in the package on 200,000 objects per collection,
one in ten surviving, it was the walk and not the call: **about 30 ns per dead object before and 3–7
after, and no difference between a kind with a finalizer and one without** on either side. Every one
of slate's hot kinds has a finalizer (`finalize_object` hands an entry table back to the spares), so
a skip-the-call change would have reached nothing here; the pointer chase reached every collection.

## The witness

In the package, `sh/sysl/gc/tests.sysl` (49 tests, 44 before): a kind with no finalizer is swept and
its storage handed back with `used` unchanged; a finalizer is called exactly once per dead object and
handed that object, including the objects at the block's base and against the bump pointer; four
kinds dying in one sweep, each finalized by its own kind, with `dispose` afterwards reaching each
survivor once (the relinked list); every freed cell handed back by the allocator exactly once; and a
weak reference that survives a sweep of its neighbours still found by the next weak pass. Skipping the
finalizer call in the sweep reddens four of them.
