# slate 0.1.35: sysl 0.8.13 and 0.8.14, and gc 0.2.10

Every binary is a PGO build from `bench/pgo.sh`, and each comparison is `bench/alternate.pl 5`: the two
binaries run alternately, program by program, and the lowest of five rounds is kept. Times are
milliseconds, all taken under `caffeinate`, at 91.5% idle before and 92.4% after.

The source is 0.1.34's. Three things changed under it:

- **sysl 0.8.13 and gc 0.2.10** (`7b9e427`). gc 0.2.9 refuses an allocation request it cannot
  satisfy and 0.2.10 aligns every payload to 16 bytes. Neither moved the time: 0.1.34 against this
  build was -0.09% on its own.
- **sysl 0.8.14** stops a statement that handles a `Result` from making two library calls on its
  success path. `mapset` is the program it reaches.

- **`mapset` -8.9%** is 0.8.14's change.
- **`alloc` +2.4%** arrived with gc 0.2.9. Most of it is not gc work: from 0.2.9 the PGO build
  inlines the collection into `new_object_on`, whose prologue then saves ten registers on every
  allocation instead of five. Keeping `collect_now` out of line takes back 1.9% of instructions and
  measured +1.8% on `alloc`'s time and +1.10% on the geomean, so it was not landed.
- **`calls` +2.1% and `methods` +0.5%** are unexplained and do not show in the time.
