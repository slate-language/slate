# 2026-09-24 — A LOCAL IN FRONT OF A REMAINDER IS ONE DISPATCH, AND A SITE'S BUILTIN IS ANSWERED IN THE CALL

`mapset` was the largest gap left to Lua after `mapset-chain`: **2.4x** (slate 41.7 ms, Lua 17.7,
LuaJIT's interpreter 13.6). Its loop is `m.set(i % 1000, i)` and `s.add(i % 1000)` two million times,
nine instructions a turn. Sampled on a PGO build (`xctrace` Time Profiler over the loop run a hundred
times longer, leaf addresses bucketed against `otool -tv` of `run_frames`):

- **`run_frames` 75%, the two natives' bodies 25%** (`Map.set` 14%, `Set.add` 11%). The natives are
  already tight — about seventy machine instructions each, the table probe one multiply, one shift
  and one compare — so nothing there was worth a change of its own.
- **Inside `run_frames`, the dispatch head was the largest single place: ~18%** — the load of the
  next instruction's tag and the indirect branch, paid nine times a turn.
- **`SlotIntRem` ~11%**, nearly all of it the hardware divide. The multiply-by-reciprocal fold was
  refuted this morning (`2026-09-24-rem-literal.md`) and was left alone.
- **The builtin call path ~25%**: the site's kind check, the plan (`placing` → out-of-line
  `placed_call` → `Builtin`), `standing_native`'s window, the indirect call through the native table,
  and the result test. No general machinery is left in it; what was left was the out-of-line call
  and a plan built only to be matched.

What changed, in the order it was measured:

1. **A site's builtin is answered inside the call instruction.** `builtin_from_site`'s hit — a kind
   code, one field read where the site entry stands, a compare — is `@inline`, the miss
   (`builtin_missed`) is `@noinline`; and `placing` answers `Native(f)` with `Builtin(f)` itself,
   so neither `CallMethod` nor `CallFn` leaves the loop for `placed_call` to call a builtin.
   *`mapset` −2.3%, geometric mean −0.12% (noise) on its own.*
2. **`LoadSlot(a); LoadSlot(i); PushInt(v); Rem` is one instruction**, `LoadSlotSlotIntRem(a, i, v)`
   (`fused_load_slot_rem` in `emit.sysl`, `fused_load_slot_int_rem` in `run_fused.sysl`): the local
   pushed and the remainder over it. It is `o.m(i % n)`, `xs[i % n]`, `[x, i % n]` — a receiver or an
   operand in front of a ring index — and folds only where both slots fit sixteen bits and the
   literal thirty-two. The `SlotIntRem` it covers is folded where it stands, so a jump landing on
   `LoadSlot(i)` (`[c && a, i % 3]` with `c` false) runs the remainder alone. **`mapset` goes 9
   instructions a turn to 7** — 18,011,037 → 14,011,037, `LoadSlot` 4,001,001 → 2,001,001,
   `LoadSlotSlotIntRem` 0 → 4,000,000.

Not built: a `CallMethod` that reads its receiver from a slot, which would drop one more dispatch
from every method call on a local. It moves the receiver's read after the arguments, so it needs a
proof that no argument assigns the local, and a second copy of the hundred-line call arm in
`run_frames.sysl`; and after step 2 the two dispatches it removed were worth less than their share of the
instruction count suggested — the call, not the dispatch, is now what a turn costs.

Control: a detached PGO build of dev `f6a68f7`; branch `mapset-2` at its measured commit. Both
`bench/pgo.sh`, sysl 0.0.134.

## The witness

`SLATE_PROFILE` on a `--features profile` build: `mapset` executes **14,011,037** instructions
against 18,011,037 on dev, the loop's trigrams now `TickSlotIntLess LoadSlotSlotIntRem LoadSlot`,
`LoadSlotSlotIntRem LoadSlot CallMethodKept` and `LoadSlotSlotIntRem CallMethodKept`.
`bench/check.sh pgo/slate`: all runtimes' answers unchanged.

Tests: `tests_mapset_2.sysl` — one site meeting a map, a set, an object with its own `has` and a map
again answers each its own; a site that knows a map still refuses an array in the tables' sentence;
every kind of key through one site (`1`/`1.0` one key, two equal objects one key) is the key the
table finds; a map and a set grown past their first size mid-loop keep every key; `longest_probe`
under 16 for the `i % 3000` fill, with a control bound; the fold is present with its covered
`SlotIntRem` standing behind it; a jump landing inside the run answers as unfolded; a folded
remainder by zero is refused at `i % 0`; a real and text are refused in the unfolded sentences.
`tests/lang/mapset2.sl` asks the same of both back ends.

## What is left

- **`mapset` is 2.2x Lua.** A turn is two builtin calls through the site cache, the window and an
  indirect call, and two hardware divides; Lua's is two table stores in its own opcode.
- **A method call on a local still pushes its receiver as an instruction of its own** — the item
  not built above.
