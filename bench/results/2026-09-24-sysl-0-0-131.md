# 2026-09-24 — sysl 0.0.131: the `Buf.push` stall is gone, and the floor moves for speed

**Two compiler releases, one story.** sysl 0.0.130 gave a generic struct an invariant the optimiser
may assume, which makes `Buf.at` one compare — and on slate it made the interpreter **slower**. 0.0.131
fixes the cause, a store-forwarding stall in the library's `Buf.push`, and the same tree built by it
runs **−5.47% and −5.54%** on the geometric mean against 0.0.129 (two alternating best-of-9 runs, PGO
against PGO). `package.hocon`'s floor moves to **0.0.131** and both `SYSL_VERSION` pins with it.

## 0.0.130: fewer instructions, more cycles

Measured on branch `sysl-130` (`f23bf39`, merged here for its record and never landed on its own;
the full tables are [2026-09-24-sysl-0-0-130.md](2026-09-24-sysl-0-0-130.md)) against the same dev `2a4c8a1` built by
0.0.129: **+2.10% and +2.93%** PGO against PGO, **+5.31%** plain against plain. The PGO binary retired
0.2–8.4% fewer instructions on twenty of twenty-three programs and still lost the time: `globals` ran
3.83 G instructions in 454 M cycles under 0.0.129 and 3.64 G in 565 M under 0.0.130 — IPC 8.4 to
6.4, and **+31.9% / +37.8%** wall. `funcs` was +16.1% both runs; `methods` and `options` were the
only large wins (−7.5% to −8.5%).

The cause was on the sysl side: under the new invariant the library's `Buf.push` stalled on store
forwarding, on the hottest path the interpreter has (every operand-stack push goes through it).
**0.0.131's library `push` reads `count` once and stores it last**, and the stall is gone; the
one-compare `Buf.at` stays.

**The instructions and the cycles now move the same way everywhere but `globals`**, which is the
witness that the stall is gone: under 0.0.130 every one of these retired fewer instructions and most
took more cycles. `globals` still retires 3.8% fewer and spends 1.7% more — a residue of the same
shape, much smaller, and the one row to start from if the sysl side looks again.

**`run_frames` is larger**: 58,376 bytes (14,594 instructions) under 0.0.129 and 65,580 bytes
(16,395) under 0.0.131, measured symbol to next symbol — about what 0.0.130 made it (16,304).

## What this means

- **The floor moves to 0.0.131** (`package.hocon`), with both CI pins. The reason is speed alone: an
  older compiler builds this same source about 5.5% slower, and 0.0.130 builds it slower still.
- 0.0.130 is the one compiler slate should not be built with; the floor now excludes it.
- `globals` is the only program still behind 0.0.129, by 2–4%.
